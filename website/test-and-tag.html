Why Test & Tag Matters

Regular testing and tagging helps identify damaged or unsafe electrical equipment before it becomes a hazard. It supports WorkSafe obligations, insurance requirements, and gives staff, tenants, and customers confidence that equipment has been checked by a professional. We provide clear reports and records so you always know what's been tested and when the next check is due.

Common Questions

Is test and tag required by law in Victoria?+

Under the Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic) and OHS Regulations 2017, employers must ensure electrical equipment is safe. WorkSafe Victoria endorses AS/NZS 3760:2022 as the accepted standard for portable appliance testing. While not named as a specific law, test and tag is effectively required to demonstrate compliance with your duty of care and is required for insurance purposes.

How often does test and tag need to be done in Victoria?+

Under AS/NZS 3760:2022, testing frequency depends on the environment: construction and demolition sites every 3 months, workshops and factories every 6 months, offices and retail every 12 months, and some low-risk stationary equipment every 5 years. Hire equipment is checked before each hire. RCD safety switches have their own push-button and trip-time schedule in AS/NZS 3760, and emergency and exit lights need a 90-minute discharge test every 6 months under AS/NZS 2293.2.

What does test and tag include?+

ZoltanStudios test and tag includes: visual inspection of equipment, earth continuity testing, insulation resistance or leakage testing, polarity testing of extension leads and power boards, pass/fail tags applied to each item, a digital compliance report emailed same day, and an AS/NZS 3760:2022 certificate. RCD tests and emergency light tests are logged on the same certificate.

Can you test RCD safety switches on the switchboard?+

Yes. We trip-test fixed RCDs from a power point on each protected circuit at their rated current (30 mA or 10 mA), at 0° and 180°, and record the trip times in an RCD test log on your certificate. In Victoria, testing is not licensed electrical work. If an RCD fails, replacing it is, so we refer it to a licensed electrician.

Do you test emergency and exit lights?+

Yes. Emergency and exit lights need a 90-minute discharge test every 6 months under AS/NZS 2293.2, and in Victoria they are an essential safety measure the building owner must keep records for. We test each fitting, log the result for your ESM records, and refer any fitting that fails to a licensed electrician for repair. $10 per fitting +GST.

Can you test second-hand electrical goods before I sell them?+

Yes. Second-hand electrical equipment sold in Victoria should be tested to AS/NZS 5761 before sale. We test and tag each item and give you a certificate listing what passed. Anything not tested is listed separately so it can be labelled as used and untested.

Do you test medical equipment?+

No. Medical electrical equipment is tested to AS/NZS 3551 by biomedical technicians. If we find medical equipment during your test and tag, we leave it untagged and list it on your certificate so you know what to book in with a biomedical service.

Do you test and tag on construction sites?+

Yes. Construction and demolition sites follow AS/NZS 3012 as well as AS/NZS 3760: equipment is tested every 3 months and tagged with that quarter's colour. We test tools, leads, power boards and site RCDs, and give you a certificate for your site records.
